# LIMA Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

LIMA welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be
respectful, technically honest, and constructive. Review the work rather than
the person, explain security claims with evidence, and make room for questions
and disagreement without ridicule.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- giving actionable, specific feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting privacy, disclosure windows, and repository authorization;
- crediting contributors and upstream projects;
- helping newcomers reproduce and understand results.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, publishing private data or credentials, unsafe
vulnerability disclosure, and using project spaces to facilitate unauthorized
access or exploitation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict
participation, or ban accounts when behavior threatens people, the project, or
coordinated vulnerability disclosure. Enforcement should be proportionate,
documented privately, and free of conflicts of interest where practical.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
channel published on the owner's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must
instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will protect reporters'
privacy to the extent reasonably possible.
